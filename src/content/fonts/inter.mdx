Inter is Rasmus Andersson's neutral sans, drawn for user interfaces and first shipped in 2017.
It's the face behind a huge share of product UIs, and the reason is mostly practical: the x-height
is large, the spacing is even, and the optical size axis (14 to 32) lets the same family work for
a 13px table cell and a 32px heading without looking like two different typefaces.

Its neutrality is the trade-off. Inter doesn't have much character of its own, so if you reach for
it you'll often need something else to carry the personality, a distinctive display face or a
bit of colour. Because so many sites use it, it can also feel like the default rather than a
choice. If you want the same reliability with a little more warmth, Funnel Sans is worth a look.
