DM Sans is a geometric sans drawn by Colophon Foundry and released through Google Fonts in 2019.
It started from the Latin portion of Poppins, the Indian Type Foundry design by Jonny Pinhorn, and
was reworked into a lower-contrast, more compact face with an optical size axis from 9 to 40.

The optical size axis is the main reason to reach for it: small text gets looser, sturdier forms,
and display sizes get tighter, more confident ones, all from one variable file. The geometric
construction gives it a clean, slightly rounded character, which suits marketing pages and
onboarding flows well. Because it's fairly neutral, it can also sit comfortably in product UI,
though it doesn't have the same distinctiveness as a face like Bricolage Grotesque. If you want a
geometric sans with a similar practical range, Inter and Geist are the nearest neighbours.
