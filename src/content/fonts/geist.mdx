Geist is Vercel's typeface, drawn by Basement Studio and released under the OFL in 2023. It's a
clean, slightly technical sans with tight, deliberate spacing and nine weights from 100 to 900,
built for the interfaces Vercel ships: dashboards, docs, and the kind of dense settings screen
where a font either behaves or gets in the way.

The thing to know is that Geist is strongly associated with one company's product, so on a
non-Vercel site it can read as borrowed branding rather than a neutral choice. It's also a plain,
restrained face, so it won't give a page much personality on its own. Where it earns its place is
as a pair: Geist Mono was made to sit beside it, and together they give a developer tool a coherent
voice without much effort.
