Schibsted Grotesk is the house typeface Bakken & Bæck designed for Schibsted, the Nordic media
and marketplace group. Henrik Kongsvoll led the drawing, and it shipped on Google Fonts in
February 2023 under the OFL. It's a grotesque built digital-first: six weights from 400 to 900
with a matching italic, tuned for product UI and editorial use across Schibsted's news and
classifieds brands before it was ever meant to sit on a poster.

Because it was drawn for interfaces, it reads as a capable, slightly anonymous workhorse rather
than a typeface with a strong personality of its own. For more visual character at display
sizes, try Bricolage Grotesque. The variable range
does mean one import covers both body copy and headlines without a second font request.
