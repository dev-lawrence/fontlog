This is the font I reach for without thinking, which is exactly why it belongs at the top of a
site about not doing that. It's a grotesque with deliberately uneven details — the flared
terminals, the slightly squared curves — that give headlines personality without tipping into
novelty. The optical size axis genuinely helps: crank `opsz` up for big display type and the
letterforms open out.

Where it bites you: at body sizes and regular weight it can feel a little stiff and wide, so I
usually pair it with something calmer for running text. It also has no italic, which occasionally
matters.
