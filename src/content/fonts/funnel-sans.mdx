Funnel Sans is the text companion to Zetafonts' Funnel display family, and it's what Fontlog
itself is set in — so I've been staring at it for a while. It's a gently geometric sans with soft,
slightly rounded terminals that keep it warm at UI sizes without looking childish. The 300–800
range covers most needs, and there's a real italic.

It's newer and less battle-tested than something like Inter, so language coverage and hinting on
Windows are worth checking for a text-heavy build. There's also a matching Funnel Display for
headlines if you want more contrast.
