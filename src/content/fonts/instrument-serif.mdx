Instrument Serif is the font to reach for when a short headline needs some presence
without getting heavier. Its narrow shapes leave room for longer titles, while the
contrast between thick and thin strokes gives large type a delicate, editorial feel.
Try it above Inter or Geist body copy on a portfolio or a landing page.

It comes in one weight, 400, with a matching italic. There is no bold or variable
axis to tune, so build hierarchy with size and spacing. Keep it for headings and
short statements: those fine details need room, and a text serif such as Crimson
Text is a better choice for a long article.
