Poppins is Indian Type Foundry's geometric sans, first published in 2015. Jonny Pinhorn designed
the initial Latin letters and Ninad Kale designed the Devanagari, with the ITF team later
expanding the family. Both scripts share circular construction and nearly even stroke widths.

Its round forms give headings a friendly, distinct rhythm, especially at medium and bold
weights. The family spans nine static weights from Thin to Black, with matching italics.
It is a useful choice for a bilingual Latin and Devanagari identity or a landing page that
needs a clear geometric voice.

That geometry can feel repetitive in long passages. Try the reading preview before choosing
it for an entire article, or pair it with Crimson Text for a more varied editorial texture.
DM Sans offers a related direction if you want a less overtly circular feel.
